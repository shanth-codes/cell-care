1. Overview & Data Controller

CELL CARE ("we", "our", or "us"), located at 21, S Veli St, South Gate, Periyar, Madurai Main, Madurai, Tamil Nadu 625001, operates an independent repair service laboratory for Apple devices. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal information of our clients in strict compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and relevant international data privacy principles.

2. Data We Collect & Why

We strictly practice data minimization. We only collect the necessary information required to communicate with you regarding your repair request:

  • Contact Details: Your name and phone / WhatsApp number (+91).
  • Device Specifications: Device model (e.g. iPhone, iPad) and reported hardware defect symptoms.

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or share your contact information with any third-party marketing companies.

3. Device Storage & Customer Personal Data Policy

During standard hardware repairs (such as display replacement, battery replacement, camera repair, or chassis alignment), your photos, messages, and internal data are mostly NOT erased. However:

  • Data will only be erased or inaccessible if the device has an underlying, unrecoverable motherboard hardware fault or NAND storage failure.
  • Customers are strongly encouraged to perform an iCloud or computer backup prior to submitting any device for diagnostics or repair.
  • CELL CARE does not browse, extract, copy, or retain customer files, photos, or personal data.
