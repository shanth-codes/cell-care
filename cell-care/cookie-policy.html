Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are used to ensure core technical website functionality.

2. How CELL CARE Uses Cookies

We do NOT use cross-site tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or third-party marketing trackers.
We only utilize strictly essential local storage tokens to remember your cookie consent preferences so the notice does not continuously reappear during your session.

3. Managing Cookies

You can configure or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox). Disabling cookies will not affect your ability to view our services or reach out for repairs.
