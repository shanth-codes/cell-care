Apple Hardware Diagnostics

Specialized Solutions for Every Apple Glitch.

Unhurried, professional bench repair. We take the necessary diagnostic time to resolve intricate iPhone, iPad and iWatch hardware faults permanently.

Display & True Tone Restoration

Cracked glass, touch unresponsiveness, or vertical OLED lines? When fitting replacement screens, we reprogram and transfer the unique sensor chip that restores the True Tone facility. Unlike quick-fix stalls that skip this chip transfer, your screen color temperature and ambient sensors function naturally.

Standard Turnaround 2 to 3 Working Days

Battery Replacement

iPhone battery draining rapidly, thermal throttling, or shutting down at 20%? We install reliable, safe replacement batteries fitted with proper factory-spec thermal stretch-release adhesive strips—avoiding dangerous spot-welding shortcuts.

Standard Turnaround 2 to 3 Working Days

Chip-Level Motherboard Repair

iPhone completely dead, stuck in a boot loop on the Apple logo, showing "No Service", or audio IC failure? We perform microscopic circuit tracing, short-circuit thermal detection, and BGA chip micro-soldering under stereomicroscopes.

Diagnostic Timeline Subject to Diagnostic

Liquid Damage De-Oxidation

Dropped in water or liquid? Do not plug it into a charger. We open the device, perform chemical de-oxidation, clean micro-corrosion using ultrasonic baths, and replace shorted SMD capacitors before irreversible board rot occurs.

Full service for iPad Pro, iPad Air, iPad Mini, and standard series. We repair shattered glass digitizers, damaged LCD panels, bent aluminum chassis realignment, and loose Type-C / Lightning charging ports.

Our Story & Craftsmanship

Madurai’s 1st Mobile Service Centre. Serving Since 1998.

When mobile phones first arrived in Madurai in the year 1998, CELL CARE opened its doors at South Veli Street as the city’s pioneer mobile service laboratory. We did not start yesterday as a quick-fix franchise.

Over the last 28+ years, as mobile technology transformed from simple monochrome handsets to today’s high-density 10-layer iPhone motherboards and micro-miniaturized Apple Watch architectures, our bench has evolved alongside it. We have continually invested in high-precision stereomicroscopes, circuit schematics, and specialized reprogramming hardware. When you bring your iPhone, iPad, or iWatch to CELL CARE, it is diagnosed by senior technicians who have dedicated their lives to electronic micro-repair.

Honest Service Policies

Quality Bench Standards.

We believe in complete transparency about repair timelines, warranty terms, and your device's data.

Turnaround: 2 to 3 Working Days

We do not perform rushed 30-minute botch jobs. Display curing, True Tone chip reprogramming, and circuit diagnostics take proper time and parts sourcing. Unhurried precision is why our repairs last.

Warranty & Counter Verification

No warranty or guarantee is provided on repairs. We do meticulous precision work so re-repairs do not occur; however, post-repair usage is outside our control. Customers inspect and test every function at our counter upon pickup.

Data Preservation Policy

Your photos, WhatsApp chats, and personal files are mostly not erased during standard hardware fixes (screen, battery, ports). Data is only erased if there is an inherent NAND storage or CPU hardware failure.
