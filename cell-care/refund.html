Refund & Service Policy

Last Updated: September 16, 2026

1. Labor & Service Fees

Because all repairs involve dedicated bench time, physical micro-soldering, and specialized labor by experienced technicians, service fees and diagnostic labor charges are non-refundable once service has been approved and completed.

2. Hardware Components

Electronic replacement parts (including OLED display panels, battery units, charging flex docks, and IC chips) that have been installed into customer devices cannot be returned or refunded once fitted, as they are consumed and permanently calibrated to the host device.

3. Inspection on Delivery

Customers are given full opportunity to inspect, test touch responsiveness, display True Tone, audio, and device charging in-person at our studio counter prior to making payment. Acceptance of the device constitutes full agreement that the repair was executed satisfactorily.
